# Maintainers

This file explains how people take on more responsibility in panchi. It lists the
roles, the evidence needed for each one, and who holds each role today.

panchi doesn't have one successor. The work is split into five roles, and each one can be
filled (or left) on its own. Every role builds on the one before it, so nobody skips a rung.

## The ladder

| Role | What they do | Access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contributor | Sends PRs and reports issues | Fork only |
| Reviewer | Reviews PRs, triages issues, answers newcomer questions | Triage |
| Maintainer | Merges PRs and owns an area (core, visualizations, docs, or assignments) | Write and merge |
| Release manager | Runs the release workflow and co-owns the PyPI project | Tag rights, PyPI owner |
| Lead | Sets direction, keeps the relationship with instructors, settles disputes | Repo or org admin |

## What it takes to move up

These are the bars a person meets before they are offered the next role. All of them can
be checked on GitHub. They are starting points and may change as the project learns
what works.

### Contributor → Reviewer

- 3 or more merged PRs
- At least 1 review on someone else's PR that caught a real problem (a bug, a missing
  test, or a mismatch with the [philosophy](https://gustavo-galvao-e-silva.github.io/panchi/contributing/#philosophy-and-values))
- Answered at least 1 newcomer's question in an issue or PR

### Reviewer → Maintainer

- 5 or more merged PRs over at least 6 weeks, including 1 non-trivial feature or refactor
- 3 or more substantive reviews of other people's PRs
- Tests and docs with every PR
- Replies to review comments and mentions within about 3 days
- Takes feedback well and keeps PRs small and scoped

### Maintainer → Release manager

- Shadowed 1 release
- Ran 1 release while a current release manager watched
- Can explain how panchi picks version numbers and how to roll back a bad release

### Release manager → Lead

- Has run issue triage or a project meeting
- Has handled a disagreement calmly
- Has spoken with an instructor who uses panchi in a course
- Plans to stay involved for at least 3 more semesters, and wants the role

## How promotions happen

1. Contributors start the same way: a specific issue, a rough size, and a check-in date.
2. Once someone meets the bar for the next role, the lead offers it to them. You can also
   ask if you think you qualify.
3. New access starts as a trial. Triage comes first, then merge rights in one area. Each
   trial gets a review after one month.
4. Promotions are recorded in the table below, so they are public and easy to explain.
5. Once a semester, the team checks who is still active, who is graduating soon, and who
   is next.

Admin and PyPI rights come last. Moving up depends on reliability and judgment more than
on raw speed or PR count.

## Stepping back

Moving to a smaller role, or stepping away, is normal and doesn't need a reason. Tell the
lead, and your entry moves from the table above to [CONTRIBUTORS.md](CONTRIBUTORS.md).
